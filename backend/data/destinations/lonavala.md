# Lonavala

## Overview

Lonavala is a popular hill station in Maharashtra, India.

It is approximately 65 km from Mumbai and approximately 64 km from Pune.

Lonavala is suitable for weekend trips, nature trips, sightseeing, trekking and road trips.

## Attractions

Popular attractions in and around Lonavala include Bhushi Dam, Tiger Point, Rajmachi Point, Lion's Point, Karla Caves and Kune Waterfalls.

## Tiger Point

Tiger Point is a scenic viewpoint offering views of the surrounding valleys.

It is suitable for sightseeing, photography and enjoying panoramic views.

## Rajmachi Point

Rajmachi Point is known for views of Rajmachi Fort and the surrounding hills.

It is suitable for nature lovers, sightseeing and photography.

## Bhushi Dam

Bhushi Dam is a popular monsoon destination.

Visitors should be careful around strong water currents and slippery surfaces during the monsoon.

## Kune Waterfalls

Kune Waterfalls is a seasonal waterfall surrounded by greenery.

It is suitable for nature lovers and photography.

## Karla Caves

Karla Caves are ancient rock-cut Buddhist caves located near Lonavala.

They are suitable for sightseeing, history and photography.

## Activities

Common activities in Lonavala include sightseeing, hiking, trekking, photography, visiting caves, exploring viewpoints and enjoying local food.

## Safety

The monsoon season makes the region particularly green and scenic.

Heavy rainfall can make roads, rocks and trails slippery.

Visitors should exercise caution around waterfalls, dams and other areas with strong water currents.
