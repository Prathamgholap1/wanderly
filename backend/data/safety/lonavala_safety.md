# Lonavala Safety

## Monsoon Safety

The monsoon season makes Lonavala particularly green and scenic.

Heavy rainfall can make roads, rocks and trails slippery.

Visitors should exercise caution around waterfalls, dams and areas with strong water currents.

## Bhushi Dam

Visitors should be careful around strong water currents and slippery surfaces during the monsoon.

## Waterfalls

Kune Waterfalls is a seasonal waterfall.

Visitors should exercise caution around wet and slippery surfaces.
