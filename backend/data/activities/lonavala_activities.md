# Lonavala Activities

## Nature

Lonavala is suitable for nature trips and sightseeing.

## Photography

Popular photography opportunities include scenic viewpoints, waterfalls, valleys and historic caves.

## Trekking

Lonavala is suitable for hiking and trekking.

## Sightseeing

Popular sightseeing locations include Tiger Point, Rajmachi Point, Lion's Point, Bhushi Dam, Karla Caves and Kune Waterfalls.

## History

Karla Caves are ancient rock-cut Buddhist caves located near Lonavala.
